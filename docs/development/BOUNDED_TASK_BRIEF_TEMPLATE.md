# Bounded Task Brief

Use this compact brief before delegating or changing a non-trivial issue. Do not expand scope without reclassification.

```md
Issue:
Objective and acceptance criteria:
Declared file scope:
Complexity: low / medium / high
Risk: low / correctness-state / security-architecture-persistence-migration-frozen-policy
Initial route: Commander direct / fresh Luna low / fresh Luna medium / Terra medium
Focused tests and required CI checks:
Attempt: 1 of 2
Stop condition / escalation trigger:
```

After CI, record the candidate SHA, mechanical scope-check result, actual-diff risk recheck, review requirement and outcome, CI runs, and whether extra model work changed the outcome in `USAGE_LOG.md`.
