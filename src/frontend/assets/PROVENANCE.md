# Frontend assets

`fonts/` holds self-hosted IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1, https://github.com/IBM/plex). They are served locally so the app makes no external font requests. The Medium weights (`IBMPlexSans-Medium.woff2`, `IBMPlexMono-Medium.woff2`, Latin subset) come from the `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans` and `@fontsource/ibm-plex-mono` 5.3.0 packages, under the same licence.

The earlier pixel-art robot sprite was retired with the illustrated direction (see `docs/design/DROPSCOPE_FRONTEND_DIRECTION.md`).
