# Research Office robot asset

`robot-roles.svg` is a hand-authored, original pixel-art sprite sheet created for DropScope's Research Office visual foundation. It was drawn specifically for this project and contains four transparent role variants: Commander, Evidence, Operations, and Risk, each with distinct workstation equipment. The grid-aligned shapes use crisp edges and scale cleanly at desk size; the asset is used only as a visual status aid and does not encode product behavior or authority.
